# Easter eggs

Five, and no more. Each one had to pass: *does this improve memory, story,
product, or personal connection?* Anything that only passed "it's funny" was cut.

---

### 1. The console banner
**Trigger:** open DevTools on any page.
**Why it works:** the people who open a console are engineers. It is a private
channel to exactly the right audience, and costs zero visual noise for everyone
else.

Contains: the real build facts (tests, corpus size, policy version), then one
line in his voice about what the build ran on. This is the **Red Bull** moment —
one line, no logo, no brand assets, no implied affiliation.

---

### 2. 😎 — the perfect fit
**Trigger:** a purchase that executes with drift exactly 0.0 and uses ≥95% of the
authorised envelope.
**Why it works:** it is *earned*. The revenue engine filling a ₹5,000 envelope to
₹4,998 without crossing it is the product working perfectly, and it deserves a
mark. Most journeys never see it.

---

### 3. Debug mode
**Trigger:** `` ` `` or `?debug=1`.
**Why it works:** it is a real HUD with real telemetry — engine microseconds,
clauses evaluated, catalog version, ledger chain head. The joke is that it is not
a joke; it is the tool he actually used while building. The label carries the
personality; the numbers are real.

---

### 4. "one thing i don't want to build again"
**Where:** Act V, a single collapsed line.
**Contents:** the Spotify clone, and the belief underneath it — following
someone else's blueprint isn't really his thing.
**Why it works:** it explains, in one sentence, why this project is an original
concept rather than a better-looking version of something that exists. It is the
most load-bearing personal detail in the entire experience, and it is disguised
as a self-deprecating aside.

---

### 5. The five
**Where:** five acts; five payment states.
**Why it works:** because it was already true. The payment state machine had
exactly five states before anyone thought about this. Nothing is labelled,
nothing is explained, and a reviewer who notices gets the small pleasure of
having noticed.

---

## Cut, and why

- **A cheetah anywhere.** Replaced by the live latency figure. A number is a
  better claim about speed than a picture of a fast animal.
- **Vehicles.** No natural home. Forcing them in would have been the exact
  "portfolio" energy the brief rules out.
- **The movie reference.** No place where it improves the product; including it
  anyway would be a gimmick by the brief's own test.
- **Music.** Embedding audio in a fintech demo is a hostile act.
- **A literal spider-web graphic.** The neighbourhood *is* the Spider-Man
  reference. Drawing a web on top would turn a metaphor into a costume.
- **"Red Bull, I'll build the whole software again" on the homepage.** It is a
  great line in conversation and try-hard in a hero section. It lives in the
  console, where finding it feels like finding something.
